Who I Am

Jai Singh

Product Designer · India

Hey, I’m Jai — a product designer turning complex workflows into clear, thoughtful digital experiences.

I work across enterprise UX, product strategy and design systems, bringing structure to complex operational problems. I start by understanding people, processes and constraints—not by jumping straight into screens.

At FarMart, I designed experiences spanning procurement, sales, finance and operations. Working across interconnected workflows taught me to think beyond individual features and design for the entire system.

Now I’m exploring how AI can strengthen research, design-system workflows and product development through projects like InterfaceOS and DesignOS.
